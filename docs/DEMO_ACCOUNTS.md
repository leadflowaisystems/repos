# Demo accounts — six verticals (M42)

Six fictional businesses, one per vertical pack, for showing Headway to owners
of different kinds of business. Corner Cafe remains the restaurant demo and is
not part of this set.

Everything in these workspaces is synthetic: the businesses, the owners, every
customer, every review and every figure. No real person's details are used.
Each workspace is exempt from trial expiry (`serviceExemption = DEMO`).

## Opening a workspace

- **As the demo owner:** sign in at `/login` with the demo login. Headway lands
  an owner on their own workspace, `/workspace/<clientId>`.
- **As platform staff:** open the business from the client list, or go to
  `/workspace/<clientId>` directly — administrators can open every workspace.

## What each workspace shows

Every story follows the same honest arc, told in the order it happened between
June and September 2026:

1. **Early feedback and a first check-in (July)** — what customers love, and
   the problem that keeps coming up.
2. **The owner talks it through** — what they told Headway (busiest times,
   the main worry, one constraint), recorded as owner context.
3. **Headway suggests, the owner decides, the change is made (early August)**
   — through the real improvement state machine. The QR card goes out the same
   week, so first-party feedback starts arriving.
4. **More feedback and a second check-in (September)** — so Trends compares two
   real periods.
5. **The before/after comparison** — computed by the product's own measurement
   rules, and the owner's note on what to do next.

About 52 pieces of feedback per business (39 public reviews and 13 QR
submissions, some with words, some taps only, one a rating alone), all read by
the deterministic reader. No AI provider is called.

| Business | Needs attention (Home) | Going well | Before/after result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Harmony Care Clinic | Appointment / booking problems — getting worse | Doctor's care; friendly staff; clean clinic | Waiting time: less often after the change (54% → 7%) |
| BrightPath Academy | Batch too large — getting worse | Strong teaching; supportive faculty | Schedule changes: less often (46% → 4%) |
| Forge Fitness Studio | Broken equipment — getting worse | Trainer knowledge; motivating atmosphere | Crowding: less often (50% → 4%) |
| Nestora Properties | Slow or no response — about the same | Honesty; local market knowledge | Slow response: about as often (42% → 39%) |
| Aura Studio & Spa | Appointment problems — getting better, still the top complaint | Stylist skill; warm staff; hygiene | Appointment problems: less often (48% → 18%) |
| EverAfter Weddings | Poor communication — getting worse | Final output; calm, professional team | Communication: more often (42% → 54%) |

## Provisioning, and running it again

```bash
node scripts/backup-production.mjs
node scripts/verify-production-backup.mjs backups/prod-<stamp>
node scripts/provision-demo-verticals.mjs backups/prod-<stamp> --admin-email <platform admin>          # dry run
node scripts/provision-demo-verticals.mjs backups/prod-<stamp> --admin-email <platform admin> --yes    # provision
```

- **Idempotent.** A business is found by the `[headway-demo:<key>]` marker in
  its operator notes. A second run creates nothing: no second business,
  feedback page, login, membership, feedback or improvement.
- **Stays in its namespace.** Only businesses carrying the marker, and users
  with a `demo.*@headway.demo` login, are ever written. A business that only
  shares a demo's name is refused, not adopted.
- **Logins** need `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (environment or `.env.local`).
  Without it the workspaces are still provisioned and the run reports that no
  login was created; running it again later with the key adds the logins.
- **Rebuilding one story** (its business, page and login are kept):
  `--reseed clinic`. Only that demo's feedback, check-ins, improvements,
  minutes and owner context are replaced.
- **Rehearsal** against a local copy: `--rehearsal-loopback` with
  `REPOS_REHEARSAL_URL` pointing at a loopback database. Supabase is never
  called in a rehearsal.

The stories live in `scripts/demo/verticals/`, the registry in
`scripts/demo/verticals/index.ts`, and the provisioning in
`scripts/demo/provision.ts`. `tests/m42.vertical-demos.test.ts` holds each
story to the feedback blueprint and proves the provisioning is idempotent and
tenant-isolated.
